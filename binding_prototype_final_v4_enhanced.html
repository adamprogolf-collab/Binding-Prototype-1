This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is made between the Client and the Service Provider. The Service Provider agrees to perform the services described herein with reasonable skill and care. Confidential information shall not be disclosed without prior written consent. Payment shall be made within thirty (30) days of invoice. Governing law shall be that of the United Kingdom. Termination conditions and penalties shall be agreed upon by both parties.

Additional clauses and terms apply as per the attached schedule.
